Teaching

I enjoy helping students make sense of ideas they find difficult. My teaching centers on rigorous reasoning, active practice, and accessible support. I want students to explain economic arguments, examine assumptions, and interpret results. Students bring different levels of mathematical and programming preparation, so I provide several ways to revisit foundations and ask questions.

At Binghamton University, I taught Statistical Methods in Summer 2025. My teaching assistantships span introductory and intermediate microeconomics, Principles of Macroeconomics, econometrics, and data analytics. In Introduction to Econometrics, I also lead R instruction.
